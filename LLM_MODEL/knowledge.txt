DWJD - DON'T WASTE JUST DONATE
================================

PROJECT OVERVIEW
DWJD (Don't Waste, Just Donate) is a web-based donation and delivery management platform that connects donors, delivery riders, and recipients. It aims to reduce food wastage by helping surplus food and essential items reach people in need through a secure, verified workflow.

WHO USES DWJD?
1. User (Donor) - Donates food or other items.
2. Rider - Picks up and delivers donations.
3. Admin - Manages the platform.

HOW TO SIGN UP
1. Open https://dwjd.vercel.app/
2. Click Sign Up.
3. Enter your name, email, and password, and select User or Rider.
4. A one-time password (OTP) is sent to your email.
5. Enter the OTP to verify your account.
6. After verification, log in.

HOW TO LOG IN
1. Open https://dwjd.vercel.app/
2. Navigate to Login.
3. Enter your email and password.
4. Select the correct role (User or Rider).
5. Click Login to open your dashboard.

HOW DOES DONATION WORK?
1. The donor logs in and opens the Donate section.
2. The donor enters item type, item name, quantity, and pickup location.
3. The system generates an OTP and emails it to the donor.
4. A rider views available pickups and locks one.
5. The rider visits the pickup location and asks the donor for the OTP.
6. The rider enters the OTP to verify and confirm pickup.
7. The rider delivers the item and marks it as delivered.
8. The donation status updates.

DONATION STATUS FLOW
Not Picked -> Picked -> Delivered

WHAT CAN A DONOR DO?
- Sign up and verify email.
- Log in.
- Submit donation requests with item details.
- View donation history.
- Track donation status.
- Apply for jobs listed on the platform.

WHAT CAN A RIDER DO?
- Sign up with location access.
- Log in.
- View available pickup requests.
- Lock a pickup so another rider cannot take it.
- Verify the donor's OTP before pickup.
- Mark a donation as delivered.
- View assigned and completed rides in My Rides.

WHAT CAN AN ADMIN DO?
- Log in using predefined credentials.
- View dashboard statistics.
- View total users, riders, donations, delivered items, and rejected pickups.
- Create and manage advertisements.
- Add job listings.
- Review job applications and schedule interviews.
- Select candidates and create employee accounts.
- Manage daily to-do lists.
- Monitor overall platform health.

HOW OTP VERIFICATION WORKS
- The OTP is a six-digit one-time password.
- It is emailed to the donor when a donation is created.
- The rider enters the OTP at pickup to confirm the donation.
- The OTP expires after 10 minutes.
- The rider cannot proceed without the correct OTP.
- OTP verification helps prevent unauthorized pickups.
- OTPs are deleted after use, according to the project description.

WHAT ITEMS CAN BE DONATED?
- Food: cooked meals, packaged food, and groceries.
- Clothes.
- Other essential items.

HOW TO TRACK A DONATION
1. Log in.
2. Open My Requests.
3. View the current status of each donation: Not Picked, Picked, or Delivered.

HOW THE JOB MODULE WORKS
1. The admin creates job listings.
2. Users view available jobs and apply.
3. The admin reviews applications and may schedule interviews.
4. The admin can reject or select candidates.
5. Selected candidates receive login credentials by email.

ADVERTISEMENT MODULE
- Admins can create promotional advertisements.
- Advertisements are shown to users after login.
- Only active advertisements are displayed.
- Admins can control advertisement visibility.

TECHNOLOGY STACK
- Frontend: React.js with Vite
- Backend: Node.js and Express.js
- Database: MongoDB with Mongoose
- Authentication: JSON Web Tokens (JWT)
- Password security: bcrypt hashing
- Email service: Brevo API
- AI assistant: Groq LLM (LLaMA 3)
- Frontend hosting: Vercel
- Backend hosting: Render
- Database hosting: MongoDB Atlas

SECURITY FEATURES
- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and are not stored as plain text.
- JWTs are used for session management and expire after seven days.
- OTP verification helps ensure only authorized riders can pick up donations.
- Role-based access control separates user, rider, and admin features.
- Input validation is enforced on API endpoints.
- OTPs expire after 10 minutes and are deleted after use.

COMMON ISSUES AND SOLUTIONS

Login not working?
- Check the email and password.
- Make sure the correct role is selected.
- Confirm that the account has been verified by OTP.
- Try logging out and logging in again.

Signup not working?
- Check that all required fields are filled in.
- Check your email for the OTP.
- OTPs expire after 10 minutes; request a new one if needed.
- Make sure the password and confirmation password match.

OTP not received?
- Check the spam or junk folder.
- Wait a few seconds and check again.
- Request a new OTP from the platform.

Donation not showing for a rider?
- Confirm that the donation status is Not Picked.
- Refresh the available pickups page.

Rider cannot complete delivery?
- Confirm that the donor's OTP is correct.
- The OTP must be verified before continuing the workflow.
