📌 PROJECT TITLE

DWJD – Don’t Waste, Just Donate

1️⃣ INTRODUCTION

DWJD (Don’t Waste, Just Donate) is a full-stack, role-based web application designed to reduce food waste and connect surplus food providers with people in need. The platform bridges the gap between donors, volunteers (riders), and administrators by using real-time coordination, authentication, OTP verification, and intelligent automation.

The project focuses on:

Reducing food wastage

Enabling transparent donation flow

Providing job opportunities

Managing logistics using riders

Offering AI-based assistance for users

Giving admins full operational control

DWJD is built using modern web technologies and follows real-world system architecture.

2️⃣ PROBLEM STATEMENT

Every day:

Tons of food are wasted

Many people go hungry

Donors lack an easy system to donate surplus food

Volunteers are not efficiently assigned

Existing solutions are either:

DWJD solves this problem with a centralized, intelligent, and secure platform.
