DWJD - DON'T WASTE JUST DONATE
================================

WHAT IS DWJD?
DWJD (Don't Waste, Just Donate) is a web-based donation and delivery management platform that connects donors, delivery riders, and recipients. It reduces food wastage by allowing surplus food and items to reach people in need through a secure and verified workflow.

Live platform: https://dwjd.vercel.app/

---

WHO ARE THE USERS?
There are 3 types of users on DWJD:
1. User (Donor) - People who donate food or items
2. Rider - Delivery volunteers who pick up and deliver donations
3. Admin - Manages the entire system

---

HOW TO SIGN UP?
1. Go to the DWJD website and click Sign Up
2. Fill in your name, email, password, and select your role (user or rider)
3. An OTP will be sent to your email
4. Enter the OTP to verify your account
5. Once verified, you can login

---

HOW TO LOGIN?
1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Select your role (user or rider)
4. Click login
5. You will be redirected to your dashboard

---

HOW DOES DONATION WORK?
1. User logs in and goes to the Donate section
2. User fills in donation details like item type, item name, quantity, and pickup location
3. System generates an OTP and sends it to the donor's email
4. Rider views available pickups and locks one
5. Rider goes to pickup location and asks donor for OTP
6. Rider enters OTP to verify and confirm pickup
7. Rider delivers the item and marks it as delivered
8. Donation status updates automatically

Donation status flow: Not Picked → Picked → Delivered

---

WHAT CAN A DONOR DO?
- Sign up and verify email
- Login to the platform
- Submit donation requests with item details
- View all their donation history
- Track donation status in real time
- Apply for jobs on the platform

---

WHAT CAN A RIDER DO?
- Sign up with location access
- Login to the platform
- View all available pickup requests
- Lock a pickup so no other rider can take it
- Verify donor OTP before pickup
- Mark donation as delivered after completing delivery
- View all assigned and completed rides in My Rides section

---

WHAT CAN AN ADMIN DO?
- Login using predefined credentials
- View dashboard with system statistics
- See total users, riders, donations, delivered items, and rejected pickups
- Create and manage advertisements
- Add jobs for people to apply
- Review job applications and schedule interviews
- Select candidates and create employee accounts
- Manage daily To-Do lists
- Monitor overall platform health

---

HOW DOES OTP VERIFICATION WORK?
- OTP is a 6-digit one-time password
- It is sent to the donor's email when a donation is created
- Rider must enter this OTP at the time of pickup to confirm the donation
- OTP expires after 10 minutes
- Without correct OTP, rider cannot proceed with delivery
- This prevents unauthorized pickups

---

WHAT ITEMS CAN BE DONATED?
- Food (cooked meals, packaged food, groceries)
- Clothes
- Other essential items

---

HOW TO TRACK MY DONATION?
- Login to your account
- Go to My Requests section
- You can see the current status of all your donations
- Status will show: Not Picked, Picked, or Delivered

---

HOW DOES THE JOB MODULE WORK?
- Admin creates job listings on the platform
- Users can view available jobs and apply
- Admin reviews applications and can schedule interviews
- Admin can reject or select candidates
- Selected candidates receive login credentials via email

---

WHAT IS THE ADVERTISEMENT MODULE?
- Admin can create promotional advertisements
- These ads are shown to users after they login
- Only active advertisements are displayed
- Admin can control visibility of each advertisement

TECHNOLOGY USED IN DWJD
- Frontend: React.js with Vite
- Backend: Node.js and Express.js
- Database: MongoDB with Mongoose
- Authentication: JWT (JSON Web Token)
- Password Security: bcrypt hashing
- Email Service: Brevo API
- AI Assistant: Groq LLM (LLaMA 3)
- Frontend Hosting: Vercel
- Backend Hosting: Render
- Database Hosting: MongoDB Atlas

---

SECURITY FEATURES
- Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt and never stored as plain text
- JWT tokens are used for secure session management and expire after 7 days
- OTP verification ensures only authorized riders can pick up donations
- Role-based access control prevents users from accessing admin or rider features
- Input validation is enforced on all API endpoints
- OTPs expire after 10 minutes and are deleted after use

COMMON ISSUES AND SOLUTIONS

Login not working?
- Check your email and password are correct
- Make sure you selected the correct role
- Check if your account is verified via OTP
- Try logging out and logging in again

Signup not working?
- Make sure all fields are filled in
- Check your email for the OTP
- OTP expires in 10 minutes, request a new one if needed
- Make sure your password and confirm password match

OTP not received?
- Check your spam or junk folder
- Wait a few seconds and check again
- Request a new OTP from the platform

Donation not showing for rider?
- Make sure the donation status is Not Picked
- Refresh the available pickups page

Rider cannot complete delivery?
- Make sure the OTP from the donor is correct
- OTP must be verified before marking as delivered
